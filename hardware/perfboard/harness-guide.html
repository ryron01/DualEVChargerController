Rules for every cable

  1. Match wires by pin name, never by position. Two of the board connectors run in the opposite order to the module they feed.
  2. Every cable is straight through. TX and RX are already crossed on the board, so no cable swaps them.
  3. Use 22–24 AWG stranded wire, and keep these cables clear of the mains wiring and CT leads, crossing them at right angles where they have to cross.
  4. Label both ends of each cable (PZEM 1, Relay 2…). The PZEM and relay cables look alike.
  5. Test each cable with a meter before plugging in a module. Every card ends with its test.
